FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free? +

Yes, 100% free! You can use Google Gemini’s generous free cloud tier (up to 15 requests per minute with zero API cost) or run completely locally with Ollama without any subscriptions or external fees.

Which AI engines are used? +

For cloud processing, we utilize Google Gemini (such as Gemini 2.0 Flash or Flash-Lite). For offline privacy, we connect directly to your local Ollama instance running Qwen 2.5 (3B for high speed or 7B for maximum depth).

How long does it take? +

With cloud Gemini, a 1-hour lecture is typically transcribed, structured, and compiled into a study book in approximately 2 to 3 minutes. With local Ollama CPU inference on a laptop, a 5-minute video takes around 5 to 10 minutes depending on your CPU core count.

What video sources work? +

You can paste video links from YouTube, Vimeo, educational portals, or direct MP4/WebM URLs. You can also upload files in MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, MP3, WAV, or M4A format up to 500 MB.

Will the PDF always be perfect? +

No AI system is infallible. While the speech-to-text and structuring are remarkably thorough, you should always review the generated notes and double-check mathematical calculations against your course syllabus before using them for exams.

Is my data stored? +

All uploaded files and temporary audio are automatically deleted from the server after 24 hours. When using Local Mode with Ollama, no audio, transcripts, or notes ever leave your personal computer.

Copyright and fair use +

LectureNotes AI is strictly intended for personal, educational, and research study purposes under fair use doctrine. Users are responsible for complying with video platform terms of service and respecting lecture copyright.
